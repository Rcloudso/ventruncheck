<div class="callout warn">

**Red-flag stop conditions — do not start cleaning (and do not keep running the dryer) if any of these are true:**

- Burning smell, smoke, or scorched/brown lint behind the dryer or at the outdoor cap
- Dryer cabinet or exhaust air much hotter than usual
- Melted plastic, crushed foil, or vinyl hose you cannot safely replace before work
- Gas dryer: gas odor when you move or approach the machine (leave the area and follow your gas utility’s emergency guidance)
- Roof, second-story, or ladder-only termination you cannot reach from solid ground — schedule a pro instead of improvising access
- Flames, glowing embers, or dense smoke — leave and follow local emergency guidance

</div>

## Numbered steps

<div class="checklist-print">

1. **Decide DIY vs pro first** — Short, reachable runs with a ground-level wall cap are DIY-friendly for many homeowners. Long hidden runs, roof caps, and multi-story chases favor a [pro full-run clean](/ventruncheck/guides/diy-vs-pro-dryer-vent-cleaning). Prep still helps either path.
2. **Power and fuel** — Unplug the dryer or flip the breaker before you pull the machine or open ducts. For gas units, plan a careful slide so the flexible connector is not kinked or stressed.
3. **Clear the work zone** — Move laundry baskets, rugs, and clutter. Give yourself a straight pull path so you do not crush the transition duct against the wall.
4. **Gather tools (DIY)** — Shop vac with hose, flashlight, gloves, clamps, and a dryer-vent brush kit sized for your duct — not a leaf blower as the primary tool ([brush kit vs leaf blower](/ventruncheck/compare/brush-kit-vs-leaf-blower)). Have rigid or semi-rigid metal transition duct ready if foil/vinyl must go ([metal vs foil](/ventruncheck/compare/metal-transition-vs-foil-duct)).
5. **Empty the lint screen** — Clean the trap and vacuum obvious lint under/behind the dryer so it is not sucked back into the path while you work.
6. **Map the run** — Note dryer outlet → transition → wall/floor fitting → outdoor termination. If you cannot see or safely reach both ends, mark the job as partial DIY or pro-only before you start.
7. **Outdoor access check** — Confirm you can inspect the [outdoor vent cap](/ventruncheck/checklist/outdoor-vent-cap-inspection) from solid ground. Skip icy, roof, or unstable ladder work — book a pro for that termination.
8. **Photo and notes** — Snap the transition type, crushed sections, and the outdoor flap. Write today’s date and symptoms (slow dry, hot cabinet, weak outdoor air) for your own log or a pro quote.
9. **Protect surfaces** — Lay cardboard or a drop cloth where you will vacuum lint. Keep kids and pets out of the work area until ducts are reseated.
10. **Plan the finish order** — Clean or schedule the full reachable path, reseat metal transition and clamps, then complete [before you run the dryer again](/ventruncheck/checklist/before-you-run-the-dryer-again) before any heat cycle with laundry.

</div>

## Notes

- Prep is not cleaning. Weak airflow after a tidy first few feet still means an unfinished run — do not “test with more heat.”
- Long runs, roof terminations, and hidden chases are easy to under-clean. Prefer a pro when you cannot reach dryer-to-termination scope safely.
