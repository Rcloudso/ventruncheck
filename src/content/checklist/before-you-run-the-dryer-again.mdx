<div class="callout warn">

**Red-flag stop conditions — do not run the dryer if any of these are true:**

- Burning smell, smoke, or scorched/brown lint anywhere in the path
- Dryer exterior or exhaust air is much hotter than usual
- Duct disconnected, crushed flat, or melted plastic still in use
- Outdoor cap still fully blocked or flap stuck closed
- Gas dryer: you smell gas after moving the machine (leave the area, follow your gas utility’s emergency guidance)
- You only cleaned the first few feet of a long run and airflow is still poor — treat as unfinished, not “good enough”

</div>

## Numbered steps

<div class="checklist-print">

1. **Power and fuel** — Dryer unplugged or breaker off while you inspect. For gas units, confirm the flexible gas connector was not kinked or stressed when you moved the machine.
2. **Transition duct** — Behind the dryer: use rigid or semi-rigid metal, not crushed foil or vinyl. No screws poking into the airflow path if you can avoid them; use clamps as appropriate.
3. **Connections** — Duct seated on the dryer outlet and on the wall/floor fitting; clamps snug; no large gaps dumping lint under the machine.
4. **Run path** — As much of the run as you can see is clear of lint mats; no sharp kinks. If you could not reach the far end, do not assume it is clean.
5. **Outdoor termination** — Cap/flap opens with airflow; screen (if any) is not packed; no bird nest material left behind.
6. **Lint filter & cabinet** — Trap cleaned; obvious lint under/behind dryer vacuumed so it is not pulled back in.
7. **Test without laundry** — Restore power. Run air-only / timed dry a few minutes. Exhaust at the outdoor cap should feel steady and warm, not weak or scorching. No burning smell.
8. **Test with a small load** — One light load. Clothes should dry in a normal time for your machine. If not, stop and revisit length, bends, and whether you need a [pro full-run clean](/ventruncheck/guides/diy-vs-pro-dryer-vent-cleaning).
9. **Document** — Note date, what you cleaned (length), and any sections you could not reach — useful for the next service or a pro quote.

</div>

## Notes

- This checklist does **not** guarantee fire safety or code compliance. It is a practical pre-use pass after cleaning work.
- Long runs, roof terminations, and hidden chases are easy to under-clean. When airflow stays weak, call a pro rather than repeating heat cycles.
